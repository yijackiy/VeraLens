VeraLens

Explainable deepfake photo detection. A pretrained visual classifier is paired with a lightweight vision–language reasoning layer, so every verdict arrives with named forensic signals and a written explanation rather than a bare probability. Bachelor thesis project.

Pipeline

01
Face detection
InsightFace detects and crops the face to 224×224.
→
02
Visual classifier
EfficientNet-B4 produces a fake probability; Grad-CAM localises it.
→
03
Reasoning layer
Qwen3-VL-2B examines the crop and the score, returns structured signals.
→
04
Fusion
Weighted combination of both signals into one explained verdict.
